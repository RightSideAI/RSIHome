Privacy Policy

Your information stays with us.

Effective November 19, 2026

RightSide Inc never sells, rents, trades, or shares any contact information you provide. Not with partners, not with advertisers, not with list brokers. Not ever.

What we collect

We only collect contact information you choose to give us, such as your name, email address, phone number, and company. This usually happens when you email us, book a conversation, or sign up for a workshop or event.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and schedule conversations, workshops, and engagements
  • To deliver the services you've asked for
  • To send occasional follow-ups related to your request, which you can stop at any time

We don't use your information for any other purpose.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell, rent, trade, or share your contact information with anyone.
  • We don't add you to third-party marketing lists.
  • This site doesn't use advertising trackers or tracking cookies.

The only exception is if the law requires us to disclose information, such as in response to a valid court order.

Free assessment tools

The ZTI, ZSA, ZTA and LDI assessments are hosted at jerrypeterson.ai. We handle any contact information you provide there under the same promise: it is never sold or shared.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct, or delete the contact information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Just email jerry@rightsideinc.com.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. We will never change our promise not to sell or share your contact information.
